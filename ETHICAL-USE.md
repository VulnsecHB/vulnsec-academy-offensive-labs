# Ethical use

The Vulnsec Academy range contains deliberately vulnerable services and offensive-security exercises. Permission to download the materials is not permission to test third-party systems.

## Authorized use

- Run the packages on equipment you own or are explicitly authorized to use.
- Test only the IP addresses and services stated in the selected package README's **Student scope** section.
- Keep the vulnerable environments isolated from public networks.
- Stop the selected package after completing the exercise.
- Use techniques learned here only in labs, capture-the-flag environments, bug-bounty programs with applicable scope, or professional assessments with written authorization.

## Prohibited use

- Scanning or exploiting third-party systems without permission
- Exposing a vulnerable target directly to the public internet
- Reusing included training credentials against any external service
- Using the materials to disrupt, damage or gain unauthorized access
- Presenting another person's completed work as your own

The learner is responsible for understanding and following the laws, contracts and authorization boundaries applicable to their activity.
